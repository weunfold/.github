# Unfold
### Open Intelligence

**AI was born from humanity's knowledge. We believe it belongs to all of us.**

Behind its capabilities are generations of people who wrote, taught, questioned, discovered, translated, built, and shared. Intelligence carries that inheritance. Its future should be something we can shape together.

Unfold begins with a conviction: humanity deserves the means to build, understand, and improve the intelligence that will shape our lives. Our ambition is to build a path toward AGI together, with its benefits shared across humanity.

## What we stand for

**Build together.** Progress should make it easier for others to begin. Share tools, research, models, methods, and lessons that people can inspect, use, and improve. Connect independent efforts so that each contribution can help the next.

**Keep people in control.** People should be able to run and adapt their intelligence, choose what it connects to, and decide what they share. Contributing to a common future must never require surrendering private data.

**Make openness visible.** Open Intelligence is a commitment to show how things work, acknowledge their limits, and make meaningful participation possible. We want decisions, funding, and the reasons behind them to be open to scrutiny.

**Give contribution a voice.** Code is one way to contribute. Research, testing, translation, design, teaching, and care for a community matter too. We want the people doing that work, and those affected by it, to help shape its direction.

**Let improvements travel.** When someone finds a better way, others should be able to build on it. Sharing an improvement requires care: protect the people whose data helped produce it, respect consent, and review what is being released.

## An invitation

Unfold is an invitation to people and projects working toward this future. Bring what you know. Connect what you are building. Help someone else take their next step.

This is the beginning of a manifesto, open to discussion and revision. Our contribution process and governance are still being shaped. Start by [opening an issue](https://github.com/weunfold/.github/issues) or proposing a change to this text.

**Humanity gave AI its beginnings. Together, we can shape what it becomes.**
