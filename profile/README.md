# we unfold
### Open Intelligence

**AI was born from humanity's knowledge. We believe it belongs to all of us.**

we unfold is a movement for Open Intelligence: building, understanding and improving AI together, in the open, with people in control of their own intelligence and their own data.

- **Manifesto:** [weunfold.ai/manifesto](https://weunfold.ai/manifesto.html). A work in progress, open to discussion.
- **Unfold:** [weunfold/unfold](https://github.com/weunfold/unfold). An early intelligence you talk to on your Mac.

Our contribution process and governance are still being shaped. Start by [opening an issue](https://github.com/weunfold/.github/issues).
